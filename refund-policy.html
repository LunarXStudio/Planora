Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded

Because every Planora product is delivered as an instant digital download, we're unable to offer refunds once a download link has been accessed. Please review product previews carefully before purchasing.

When we will help

  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • The file you received is corrupted or won't open.
  • You purchased the wrong product by mistake and haven't yet downloaded it.

In any of these cases, contact us within 7 days of purchase and we'll make it right — with a replacement file, correction, or refund where appropriate.

How to request help

Reach out to us through the Contact section of our homepage with your order details, and we'll respond within 2 business days.

Chargebacks

We'd always rather resolve an issue directly — please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank or card provider.
